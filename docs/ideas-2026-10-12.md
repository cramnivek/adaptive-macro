# Ideas, ranked — 2026-10-12

Written overnight, for the owner to pick from. Ranked by what each one unblocks
or fixes for testers, not by how interesting it is. Each has a rough size: S is
under an hour, M is an evening, L is several sessions.

## Before more testers

1. **Your own email domain for sign-in (S, owner).** Gmail SMTP works for friends,
   but codes come from a personal address and Gmail caps it at 500 a day.
   Resend's free tier with a domain you own is a change to the same SMTP form.
2. **Per-user AI limits (M).** See
   `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-12-per-user-ai-access-design.md`. It fixes store
   blocker #1: one shared token and one shared 300-a-day ceiling.
3. **Privacy policy page (S once the draft is filled).** `docs/privacy-policy-draft.md`
   → a `/privacy` page in the web app, linked from Settings and the store listings.
   Check the Gemini free versus paid tier first: it changes what the policy says.
4. **Finish the sign-in check (S, needs you awake).** Sign out and back in, and
   delete an account, end to end with real codes. Everything else was checked in
   the browser.
5. **Deploy (S).** The web app still runs the version from before accounts and the
   rename. Deploy after 4.

## Training (what Hevy has that Ascend doesn't)

10. **Calendar of training days (M).** A month grid with the days you trained
    marked, which opens the past workout (built overnight). It's the "Calendar"
    on Hevy's profile.
11. **Weekly charts: duration, volume, reps (M).** The three tabs on Hevy's
    profile. The data is all in `sets` and `sessions`. Trends already draws
    charts for weight.
12. **Edit a past workout (M).** Fix a mistyped weight after finishing. The new
    past-workout screen is the place for it. Milestones and the estimate re-read
    sets, so nothing else needs to change.
13. **Body measurements (M).** Waist, arms, etc., beside body weight. That's the
    "Measures" on Hevy's profile.
14. **Rest timer when the phone is locked (M, native only).** On the web app the
    timer can't wake a locked iPhone. On Android a scheduled local notification
    can (`expo-notifications`).

## Smaller polish

15. **Rate RPE before ticking (S).** Asked about earlier. Today RPE appears only
    after the set is ticked, which is deliberate but surprising.
16. **The unit switch could convert to plate-friendly numbers (S).** 100 kg
    becomes 220.5 lb. Rounding to the nearest 2.5 lb (or 1.25 kg) would match
    real plates. That's a product decision: it changes what's recorded.
17. **Make the session header's KG/LBS and ALERT controls 44 px tall (S).** They're
    about 28 px, under the app's own touch-target size.
18. **Expo SDK patch update (S).** `57.0.23 → 57.0.27`, plus 10 packages, flagged
    by `expo start`. Check with `npx expo install --check`.
